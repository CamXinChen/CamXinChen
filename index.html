Email Google Scholar ORCID GitHub

I am a final-year PhD candidate at the University of Cambridge, working at the interface of electron microscopy, optoelectronic semiconductors and machine learning. I develop four-dimensional scanning transmission electron microscopy (4D-STEM) methods — including in-situ and operando techniques — to reveal, at the nano- and atomic scale, chemical composition, structure, strain and defects together with local electric fields, charge density and electrostatic potential, linking these microscopic features to the macroscopic optoelectronic behaviour and performance of working devices.

My research combines multimodal STEM (imaging, EDS, EELS and diffraction) with machine-learning pipelines that make high-throughput characterisation tractable on large, high-dimensional 4D-STEM and spectral datasets. I am supervised by Prof. Caterina Ducati, Prof. Paul Midgley FRS and Prof. Henning Sirringhaus FRS, and work within the Cambridge Electron Microscopy Group and the Cavendish Laboratory Optoelectronics Group.

Research

FIB-SEM nano-fabrication

Optimise low-damage thinning processes for TEM lamellae of beam-sensitive materials.

Multi-modal STEM

Integrate imaging, EDS / EELS / SED analyses to study composition, chemical states and structural distortion in perovskite optoelectronics — identifying causes of failure and stabilisation strategies for devices.

In-situ 4D-STEM

Develop an in-situ chip-holder system and 4D-STEM–DPC techniques to study electrical properties and charge-transport mechanisms in operando perovskite transistors.

AI4S TEM analysis

Develop machine-learning algorithms for rapid, high-throughput analysis of spectral and diffraction datasets.

Software & Data

SIGMA — ML pipeline for scientific data analysis

Lead developer. A deep-learning pipeline for automated, high-throughput curation, clustering and evaluation of large spectral and diffraction datasets. Built on py4DSTEM, it compresses high-dimensional diffraction patterns into a small number of meaningful latent dimensions for phase and orientation discovery across 10k–100k-pattern datasets, with a convolutional-VAE and Bayesian Gaussian-mixture backbone.
