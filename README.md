I work at the interface of **electron microscopy**, **optoelectronic semiconductors** and **machine learning**. My research develops four-dimensional scanning transmission electron microscopy (**4D-STEM**) — including in-situ and operando techniques — to connect atomic-scale structure, strain and defects to the electrical behaviour of working devices.

Supervised by Prof. Caterina Ducati, Prof. Paul Midgley FRS and Prof. Henning Sirringhaus FRS (Cambridge Electron Microscopy Group · Cavendish Laboratory Optoelectronics Group).

### Research focus

- **Operando & in-situ 4D-STEM** of perovskite field-effect transistors — resolving charge transport and polaron–lattice coupling in working devices.
- **Multimodal STEM** — imaging, EDS, EELS and diffraction for composition, chemical state and structural distortion.
- **Machine learning for large scientific datasets** — unsupervised representation learning for phase and orientation discovery across 10k–100k-pattern diffraction data.

### Software & data

- **SIGMA** — lead developer. Deep-learning pipeline for automated curation, clustering and evaluation of large spectral and diffraction datasets, built on **py4DSTEM**.
- Contributor to **py4DSTEM** and **HyperSpy**.
